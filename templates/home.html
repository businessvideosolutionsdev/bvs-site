How working with BVS works.

A full system.
Built around your business.

We create your ads, build the systems behind them, and work with you every week to turn more opportunities into customers.

  1. 01A plan for your business

    Understand your business.

    We map your business, sales process and existing tools through onboarding and a technical discovery call.

    What we work through

    Your offer, customers, current sales process and existing tools. Together, we identify what needs to be built so your marketing has a clear path to a sale.

  2. 02Your CRM + automations

    Build your follow-up system.

    We build your CRM, lead notifications and automated follow-up while your ads take shape.

    What’s included
    • Lead tracking and notifications
    • Email setup and automated follow-up sequences
    • Text messaging setup and registration
    • AI reply suggestions informed by your business
    • AI call answering when you’re unavailable
  3. 03Approved scripts + filming

    Plan it. Then film it.

    We shape the offer, write scripts with your approval and come to you to film.

    What to expect on shoot day

    A typical ad shoot takes about three hours. Social media content can require additional time or a separate shoot day. You come prepared, with the scripts already agreed.

  4. 04Campaigns ready to run

    Get ready. Go live.

    You approve the finished ads. We aim to launch within seven business days of filming, with your systems ready.

    How the launch comes together

    We aim to complete your CRM and automation setup before filming or final ad approval. Once the ads are approved and the systems are ready, our team uploads and launches the campaigns.

  5. 05A clear sales pipeline

    Turn inquiries into conversations.

    Your team calls, qualifies and books leads with conversations and next steps tracked in one place.

    Keep the next step clear

    Track conversations and progress in one pipeline. Automated follow-up and AI reply suggestions support your team as you work toward appointments and closed jobs.

    Appointment setting is available separately as an optional add-on.

  6. 06Weekly review + next steps

    Review. Refine. Keep improving.

    Every week, we review sales performance together and agree on the next actions for BVS and your team.

    What we focus on together

    How inquiries are moving toward appointments and closed jobs, alongside the number of leads arriving. We use what’s happening in your business to guide the next changes.

Done for you. Managed with you.
Ads, CRM, automations and ongoing support, with clear responsibilities from the start.

Talk to our team ↗
Is BVS a good fit for my business?

We work with local and service-based Florida businesses that want more qualified inquiries and customers. Home services, pools, health and wellness, automotive and other local businesses are a good starting point. We’ll talk about your offer, service area and goals to see whether we’re a fit.

Do you create the content and run the ads?

Yes. We plan the message, create ad and social content, manage Meta and Google campaigns, and review the performance. Your dedicated account manager keeps the work connected to your business goals.

Will your team come to our location?

Yes. On-site content is a core part of our approach. We focus on Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville, Sarasota, Fort Myers and Naples. Tell us where you’re based and we’ll discuss the logistics on your strategy call.

What results should I expect?

We focus on qualified leads, customer acquisition and revenue. The client stories above show specific past outcomes. Your results depend on your offer, market, budget and sales process. We’ll set realistic goals with you on the strategy call.

How much does it cost?

Your campaign plan depends on your business, location, content needs and advertising budget. We’ll discuss scope and pricing on your strategy call so you understand the investment before deciding.

What happens on the strategy call?

You’ll talk with Christian about your business, what you’ve tried and where you want to grow. We’ll look at the opportunity in your market and explain what working together could look like. There’s no obligation.
